# Monday Reporting Control Pack

## What this is

A set of simple documents for checking Monday sales reports before use and recording known copies of customer information. It requires no additional software.

## What is included

- `monday_reporting_checklist.md`: the form completed each Monday.
- `customer_data_copy_log.md`: a record of known customer data copies and how data requests are handled.
- `sample_completed_checklist.md`: an illustrative example using made-up figures.
- `improvement.md`: the purpose, estimated value, costs, and limitations of the pack.

## Who does what

These responsibilities are proposed and have not yet been formally assigned. The board should assign them to existing staff or the two approved hires, with a named backup for each role.

- **Preparer:** Completes the checklist using the Monday report.
- **Finance reviewer:** Reviews the definitions, totals, differences, and release decision.
- **Nana:** Receives the outcome and decides on escalated business risks.
- **Customer data owner:** Maintains the copy log and coordinates data requests.

Until assignments are confirmed, leave the names blank. The same person should not be assumed to hold every responsibility.

## How to use the checklist

1. Enter the reporting week, preparation date, and names of the preparer and reviewer.
2. Complete sections A to E. Tick a box only when the check passes. If it does not apply, write N/A and explain why.
3. Record errors, unexplained differences, and exceptions. Do not change source data simply to make totals agree.
4. In section F, select Ready, Ready with a documented exception, or Hold.
5. If a required check fails or a material difference remains unexplained, hold the report unless the reviewer explicitly approves a documented exception.
6. Record outstanding problems, the responsible person, and the due date.
7. The preparer and reviewer sign and date the checklist.

## What each section checks

**A. File:** Confirms the report covers the correct week and includes the expected fields.

**B. Duplicates:** Identifies repeated order numbers that could inflate order counts or revenue. Investigate a repeated number before deciding whether it is a genuine duplicate.

**C. Numbers:** Compares this week with last week. A large change is not automatically wrong, but it needs an explanation. Refunds must follow Finance's approved revenue definition. Negative stock counts require investigation.

**D. Match:** Compares Operations and Finance totals. Differences must be reconciled or documented, including any unresolved portion.

**E. Customer data:** Checks that reports contain only necessary customer details, exports are logged, files are stored appropriately, and requests are referred to the customer data owner.

**F. Decision:** Records whether the report can be used. Any approved exception must include the reviewer's written approval, the reason, and the follow-up action.

## Working definition of revenue

Proposed definition: money received from paid orders, minus refunds, counted on the day the payment arrived.

This is not yet an approved business definition. Finance must confirm it in writing before it is used as the official basis for reporting. Until then, mark revenue figures as pending Finance approval and do not present them as an agreed measure.

## Decisions needed before first use

- Finance approval of the reporting change threshold.
- Finance approval of the revenue definition.
- Assignment of the responsible people and their backups.
- Agreement on the report's storage location and access permissions.

## Using the Customer Data Copy Log

Record each known copy of customer details when it is created or before it is shared, where practicable. Include who created it, why it was needed, where it was saved or sent, and who has access.

When a data request arrives, refer it to the customer data owner. The owner identifies known copies, assesses the request under applicable requirements, coordinates necessary actions, and records the outcome and any outstanding items.

The initial task is to identify existing copies, including Marketing's customer list, Operations and Finance spreadsheets, and earlier exports. Review the log monthly for missing deletion dates and unresolved requests.

The log is not proof that every copy has been found. Data requests must be handled with appropriate review and authorization. Record any copy that remains unidentified or unresolved, together with the action needed.

## Limitations

- The checklist catches common problems but does not prove every figure is correct.
- The log records information people provide; it does not discover copies automatically.
- This pack is not legal advice and does not replace appropriate data protection guidance.
- The process is manual. The estimate of 10 minutes per week has not been tested against a real report.
- Automation is a possible Q3 activity only if Finance has approved the revenue definition, the manual checks have demonstrated practical value, and a named person is responsible for maintaining the automation. Otherwise, continue the manual process and resolve the outstanding issues first.
