# Improvement beyond the brief: Monday Reporting Control Pack

## The problem it solves

The brief leaves two things exposed.

First, nobody checks the Monday numbers before they are used. Differences between Operations and Finance are found late, in a meeting, instead of before the numbers are shared.

Second, nobody keeps a complete, verified record of customer data copies. Marketing's list alone covers about 300,000 people. With a Data Protection Commission complaint concerning deleted customer data, Kloud Kart needs a better way to identify where customer information has been stored or shared.

## What I built

A pack of simple documents that requires no software or installation.

1. **Monday reporting checklist.** Checks the reporting file, repeated orders, changes against last week, and whether Operations and Finance totals match. If a required check fails or a difference remains unexplained, the report is held unless the reviewer grants a documented exception.
2. **Customer Data Copy Log.** Records known copies of customer details, including who made each copy, why it was needed, where it was stored or sent, who else received it, and when it was deleted. It also records how data requests were handled.
3. **Worked example.** An illustrative scenario showing a report being held because of repeated orders and an unexplained difference.
4. **README.** Explains responsibilities, checks, limitations, and what must be agreed before the pack is used.

## Who does what

These are proposed responsibilities, not confirmed appointments. The board should assign them to existing staff or the two approved hires, with a named backup for each role. No additional hire is required.

- **Preparer:** Completes the checklist. Initially, this should be the person who prepares the Monday numbers.
- **Finance reviewer:** Reviews the definitions, totals, differences, and release decision.
- **Nana:** Receives the outcome and decides on escalated business risks.
- **Customer data owner:** Maintains the copy log and coordinates data requests.

## How it creates value

- Gives Nana a clear answer each Monday about whether the numbers are ready for use.
- Surfaces differences with Finance before the meeting.
- Creates a starting point for identifying customer data copies and documenting how requests are handled. The log does not prove every copy has been found.
- Provides a simple control while the underlying reporting process is improved.

Using the assumptions in the diagnosis, one hour saved each week would represent:

- **Nana:** 1 hour × GHS 100/hour × 50 weeks = **GHS 5,000 per year**.
- **Finance:** 1 hour × GHS 60/hour × 50 weeks = **GHS 3,000 per year**.

These are hypothetical scenarios, not measured savings. Time saved does not automatically translate into cash savings, and the two figures must not be treated as verified financial benefits.

## What it costs

- **Software and purchases:** GHS 0, assuming existing document tools are used.
- **Checklist time:** Estimated at 10 minutes per week, approximately 8.3 hours annually. At the assumed GHS 100/hour rate, this represents approximately GHS 833 in annual staff time. The estimate has not been tested against a real report.
- **Log maintenance:** Additional staff time whenever customer information is copied or shared, plus a monthly review.

These are estimates, not measured costs.

## Limitations and conditions

The pack does not eliminate manual reporting work, resolve every underlying data quality problem, or automatically find every copy of customer information.

Before use, Kloud Kart must confirm the reporting change threshold, approve the revenue definition through Finance, assign the responsible people and backups, and agree where the files will be stored.

Automation becomes a Q3 task only if all three conditions are met:

1. Finance has approved the revenue definition.
2. The manual checks have demonstrated practical value.
3. A named person is responsible for maintaining the automation.

If these conditions are not met, Kloud Kart should continue using the manual control and resolve the outstanding issues before investing in automation.
