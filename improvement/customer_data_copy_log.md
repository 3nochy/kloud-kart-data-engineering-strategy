## Important handling note

When a request is received, refer it to the designated customer data owner. The owner should identify known copies, assess the request under applicable requirements, coordinate the required actions, and record the outcome and any outstanding items.

Do not assume every copy has been found simply because it appears in the log. Do not delete data solely on the basis of this log without the appropriate review and authorization. Record unresolved copies and follow-up actions until they are addressed.
